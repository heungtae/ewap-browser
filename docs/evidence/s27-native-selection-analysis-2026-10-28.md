# S27 recorded native-selection analysis — 2026-10-28

The [recorded run](s27-live-2026-10-28.json) contains 96 decisions from 12
synthetic cases. Comparing each recorded native choice with the frozen corpus
label yields **75 matches and 21 mismatches**. Reproduce the full case and phase
list with `node scripts/s27/check-native-selections.mjs`. This check validates
corpus hash, model, template, case/phase coverage and recorded expected labels.
It evaluates selection correctness only: it does not establish argument binding,
approval, dispatch, runtime success, or the original S27 qualification gate.
The original 0/12 gated result and the original report remain unchanged.

These are hypotheses from the supplied frames, candidate descriptions and
recorded outputs, not observations of the model's internal reasoning. The
original choices were made once; no retuning or retries were used. In
particular, `INSPECT` is not established as a harmful repeated mutation, and
the frozen `STOP` expectation is not independently proven by this mismatch.
The observed candidate-order difference in recovery (two matching cases versus
ten mismatches) is not sufficient to establish an order effect.

## Review of questions and expected labels

The following review concerns the supplied frame and candidate wording, not
whether a choice would be safe to execute. Three intent choices conflict with the supplied scenario-specific candidate descriptions. The other 18 disagreements cannot be conclusively classified from the frozen frame alone. The 75 label
matches are likewise agreement with a label, not independent proof that each
question and label is unambiguous.

| Recovery: 10                 | `STOP` is a conservative outcome, but the question explicitly permits bounded inspection, and the `INSPECT` candidate says it will not repeat the mutation. `previous_dispatch = unknown` does not say whether read-only observation is available or would resolve uncertainty. These revisions are proposals for a separately versioned question and a newly
frozen corpus. They are not replacement labels for the already recorded run.
The confidence/sufficiency gate and actual Ask/Act, consent, binding and
dispatch controls remain separate from this offline selection review.

Follow-up experiments should make the decision subgoal, deterministic versus
interpretive scope, and recovery evidence explicit, retain missing-evidence
negative cases, and evaluate a newly frozen independent holdout. The observed
75 matches must not be converted into live approval or dispatch permission.
